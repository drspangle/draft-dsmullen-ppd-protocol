# Effective Policy Direction

This note tracks the working direction for the baseline Effective Policy Object,
including freshness semantics, taxonomy extensibility, and provenance scope.

It is intended to support coherent edits to
`draft-dsmullen-ppd-protocol.md` as topic-by-topic decisions are made.

## 2026-05-12

### Decision

Keep the baseline Effective Policy Object centered on:

- `policy_id`
- `policy_hash`
- `rules`
- exactly one of `renew_by` or `renewal_interval`
- optional taxonomy context
- optional policy-instance provenance fields

The draft should make three boundaries explicit:

- freshness fields govern association freshness, not abstract policy validity;
- taxonomy context can support company-specific taxonomies and extensions when
  they map to the core primitives and are declared properly; and
- the baseline provenance fields describe the policy instance itself, not the
  provenance of data collected or generated by participant devices.

### Why This Direction Holds

- `policy_id` plus `policy_hash` are the minimum stable identity pair for the
  thing being acknowledged
- renewal fields are needed to maintain current association, not to describe
  whether the policy text remains globally valid in a broader administrative
  sense
- organization-specific taxonomy growth is inevitable and should be supported as
  long as it remains grounded in the shared core primitives
- policy-instance provenance and collected-data provenance are both important,
  but they are different semantic concerns and should not be conflated

### Freshness Boundary

`renew_by` and `renewal_interval` are association-maintenance fields.

They answer:

- by when must this device-side participant renew or replay the protocol steps
  to keep current association

They do not answer:

- whether the household policy is still globally valid
- whether the policy source remains administratively approved
- whether participant-collected data remains usable for another purpose

### Taxonomy Extensibility Boundary

The baseline protocol should support company-specific taxonomies and extensions
when:

- their compact terms are declared through the taxonomy context when needed;
- their meaning maps back to the core shared primitives; and
- the resulting messages remain evaluable under the protocol's term-resolution
  rules.

### Provenance Boundary

The baseline provenance fields in the Effective Policy Object are:

- `base_policy_id`
- `applied_policy_id`
- `computed_at`

These fields describe the provenance of the policy instance itself.
They help explain:

- which baseline policy contributed to the effective result
- whether a more specific applied layer was involved
- when the effective instance was computed or materialized

They do not describe:

- the provenance of raw data collected by household devices
- the origin of derived data generated later by those devices or services
- lineage, transformation, or downstream handling history of collected data

Those collected-data provenance semantics are privacy-critical, but they belong
to a different part of the overall design space than the baseline
participant-facing Effective Policy Object.

### What To Avoid In The Draft

- do not describe association-freshness fields as if they were abstract policy
  validity fields
- do not block company-specific taxonomies that correctly map to the shared
  primitives
- do not let policy-instance provenance fields masquerade as collected-data
  provenance semantics
- do not overexpand the Effective Policy Object to solve all future data
  provenance needs in the baseline participant protocol

### Short Drafting Position

The shortest coherent statement of this decision is:

"The baseline Effective Policy Object identifies the policy instance to be
acknowledged, governs association freshness, and may expose limited
policy-instance provenance. Its freshness fields are about maintaining current
association, not abstract policy validity. Its provenance fields are about the
origin of the effective policy instance, not the provenance of data later
collected or derived by household devices. Company-specific taxonomies and
extensions remain in scope when they map back to the shared core primitives and
are declared through the taxonomy context."
